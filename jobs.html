We're hiring! Check below for available job opportunities.

  1. Sales Associate

    As a Sales Associate, you'll help customers turn their travel ideas into memorable experiences. You'll work with customers to give them packages and tours for their holidays and provide friendly service. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys meeting new people and has a passion for travel.

    Salary $71,000 per year
    Reporting line Reports to the Sales Manager
    Responsibilities
    • Assist customers with their purchases
    • Maintain the shop floor
    • Process sales and payments
    Essential requirements
    • Previous retail experience
    • Good communication skills
    • Ability to work as part of a team
    Preferred requirements
    • Previous experience in sales
    • Knowledge of the products

  2. Front Desk Assistant

    As a Front Desk Assistant, you'll be one of the first points of contact for our guests and visitors. You'll provide a warm welcome, answer questions, assist with bookings, and help travellers make the most of their stay. We're looking for someone friendly and enthusiastic about helping people explore new places.

    Salary $65,000 per year
    Reporting line Reports to the Front Office Manager
    Responsibilities
    • Welcome guests and assist with check-in and check-out
    • Answer enquiries about local attractions and tours
    • Handle phone calls, emails, and customer requests
    Essential requirements
    • Friendly and professional manner
    • Good communication skills
    • Basic computer skills
    Preferred requirements
    • Previous hotel, hospitality, or tourism experience
    • Knowledge of local attractions

  3. Marketing Manager

    As our Marketing Manager, you'll help inspire travellers to discover new destinations and experiences through creative marketing campaigns. You'll oversee our digital presence, and work with the wider team to showcase our tours and holiday packages. This is a great opportunity for a creative and organised marketer with a passion for tourism.

    Salary $85,000 per year
    Reporting line Reports to the General Manager
    Responsibilities
    • Develop marketing campaigns promoting tours and holiday packages
    • Manage the company's social media accounts
    • Manage the annual marketing budget
    Essential requirements
    • Degree or equivalent experience in marketing, communications, or business
    • Strong written and verbal communication skills
    Preferred requirements
    • Previous experience in marketing
    • Graphic design or video editing experience
