Tourism Association

There's a place you'll show your face

The Tourism Association would like to acknowledge the Traditional Owners of the land on which Swinburne is located, and to pay our respects to elders past, present, and emerging.

The Tourism Association is dedicated to making it easy to book accomodation, travel, and tours at the click of a button. We also have services to help you make your plans, and if you need we can also link you up with one of our planners.

The Tourism Association makes planning flights easier than the nap you'll take while on them. Flights cost as little as free, depending on the source.

Once you arrive at your destination, you are free to book a hotel room, and we will be able to find a hotel room as cheap as a bargain price per night.

Members of us get a discount of up to 100% on various services, as well as various exclusive perks.
